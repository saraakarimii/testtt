Recommended Workflow

Projects are optional during upload and only required when assembling a book.

  1. Upload Images: Drop PNG, JPG, JPEG, or WEBP files directly into Artwork Inbox.
  2. Review & Organize: Browse thumbnails, check dimensions, and assign selections to a project.
  3. Process & Tune: Compare Engines A–D and refine Notebook v2 parameters in Processing Lab.
  4. Generate Book: Approve processed artworks and build Side-by-Side and Full-Page A4 PDFs, each with its own numbered pages and matching colour answer-key pages.
